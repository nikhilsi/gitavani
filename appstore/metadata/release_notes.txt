Learn to chant

Ever wanted to chant a verse and not been sure you were saying it right? GitaVani can help with that now. Pick a verse and tap Learn. The reciter chants one line at a time and you chant it back. GitaVani listens and shows which words you got right and which ones need another try. Keep going until you can chant the whole verse from memory.

Everything happens on your iPhone. Nothing you say is saved or sent anywhere. Learn needs iOS 27 and a one-time download of Apple's Hindi speech model.

Word by word

Open any verse and tap Word by word. It sits under the Play and Save buttons. You'll see each Sanskrit word in Devanagari and Roman letters with its meaning. The meanings come from Swami Sivananda's commentary and cover 693 of the 701 verses.
