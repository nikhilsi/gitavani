GitaVani is a clean, ad-free reader for the Bhagavad Gita. It has all 701 verses in Sanskrit. Each one comes with translations and commentaries. You can also listen to it chanted.

New in 1.1: Learn to chant a verse. GitaVani listens on your phone and shows which words you got right. Also new: Word by word meanings.

FEATURES

• Sanskrit audio for all 701 verses. Follow along with the text while you listen.
• Learn (new): practice chanting any of 680 verses. The reciter chants a line. You chant it back. GitaVani listens on your phone and marks each word. You also get one tip for your next try. Finish by chanting the verse from memory. Needs iOS 27 and a one-time download of Apple's Hindi speech model.
• Word by word (new): each word of a verse in Devanagari and Roman letters, next to its meaning. The meanings come from Swami Sivananda and cover 693 of the 701 verses.
• Devanagari text with optional Roman transliteration
• Translations in Hindi and English from 11 scholars. Swami Sivananda and Sri Shankaracharya are among them. Pick a different translator on any verse.
• More than 11,000 commentaries from 17 scholars in English, Hindi and Sanskrit
• Search every verse by Sanskrit, Roman letters or translation
• Save favorite verses. Sort them by date saved or by chapter.
• Share a verse as a themed image card
• Swipe from verse to verse. Swiping carries on into the next chapter.
• Four reading themes: Sattva (light), Parchment (warm), Dusk (dark) and Lotus (saffron)
• Adjustable font size that also follows your iPhone's text size setting
• Picks up where you left off
• A short welcome tour and a help screen

PRIVACY

No ads, no tracking and no account. GitaVani collects no data. Reading and listening work offline. Learn listens on your phone and never sends audio anywhere.

DATA SOURCE

Verse data sourced from the Vedic Scriptures project by Pt. Prashant Tripathi, used under LGPL-3.0 license. Original Sanskrit shlokas are ancient public domain text.
