GitaVani is an offline Bhagavad Gita reader. All 701 verses and their Sanskrit audio (MP3) are bundled in the app. The app makes no network calls and collects no data. No account is needed.

New in 1.1 is a Word by word card on each verse. Tap it below the Play and Save buttons to see each word with its meaning. The meanings are bundled too.

To try the main features, tap any chapter, then any verse. On the verse screen, Play chants the verse, Romanize shows Roman letters, Save adds it to favorites and Share makes an image card. Swipe left or right to move between verses. Pull down on the home screen to search.
